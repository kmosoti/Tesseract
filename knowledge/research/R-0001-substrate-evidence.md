# Evidence for contextual representations and acquired abstractions

## Observed

- [Meyer and Lewis, 2020](https://aclanthology.org/2020.conll-1.21/)
  train real density matrices for lexical ambiguity. Their best model/composition
  outperforms compared baselines on three of four disambiguation datasets; word
  similarity results are mixed. This is task-specific evidence, not evidence for
  complex phases or a continuously developing general intelligence.
- [Piedeleu et al., 2015](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.CALCO.2015.270)
  develop compositional density-matrix semantics and report a small real-data
  experiment. Completely positive semantic composition does not imply every
  useful semantic operation should be a trace-preserving physical channel.
- [Quantum cognition overview, 2025](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-025-02675-9)
  surveys contextual and order effects and open modeling questions. Classical
  dynamical systems can also exhibit noncommuting operations.
- [DreamCoder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381) acquires reusable symbolic
  abstractions with program-library learning and neural-guided search. It supports
  investigating executable acquired knowledge, not assuming our simpler learner
  reproduces that system's results.
- [Vector symbolic architecture comparison](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10462-021-10110-3)
  supplies a classical alternative for distributed symbolic composition.

The Parallel deep-research action required account linking and did not produce a
research report. Public search/fetch supplied the sources above. No synthetic
performance result is presented as an experiment.

## Inferred

Separate the developmental thesis from substrate selection. Measure transfer,
persistence, retention, uncertainty, and total resource cost with matched evidence.
Representation validity alone cannot establish useful learning.

## Hypothesis

Contextual operator representations may encode transferable knowledge efficiently.
Bounded program acquisition can make experience useful beyond exact retrieval.

## Unknown

The best substrate; identifiable complex-phase contributions; useful automatic
ontology growth; scaling from controlled worlds to richer interaction. Isometric
expansion preserves state geometry but does not create a meaningful distinction.
