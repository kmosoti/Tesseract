# Workshop v1 controlled comparison

## Observed

The user chose a small workshop and required the quantum candidate in the first
runnable milestone. Independent review identified the need to distinguish learned
transition competence from causal contribution of the macro library.

## Inferred

Freeze [the protocol](../../experiments/workshop-v1.json) and
[methodology](../../docs/methodology.md) before final benchmarking. Compare three
representations on identical public experience, with fresh-object triple transfer,
actual journal restart, controls, and macro/order/phase diagnostics.

## Hypothesis

Durable transition learning reduces held-out Brier loss at least 5% over the
strongest non-developmental control, with a paired interval above zero. Macros
improve tightly bounded search. Complex channels must improve over the strongest
classical candidate and remain within the separate 2x resource gates.

## Unknown

All benchmark verdicts remain unknown until execution. Four-state classical
worlds may decisively favor the simpler representation; that is an admissible
negative substrate result. Coordinate counts are not matched intrinsic capacity.
