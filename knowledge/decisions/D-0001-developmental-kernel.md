# Developmental kernel with competing substrates

## Observed

The user requested a foundational pivot and selected quantum mathematics as an
experimental candidate, a workshop simulation, quantum inclusion in v0.1, and
automatic learn/validate/commit. They requested implementation of that plan.
The earlier Tesseract repository and experiment artifacts were unavailable.

## Inferred

Use stable identities, public evidence, inspectable transitions, acquired bounded
programs, explicit validation, persistent revisions, and rollback as the stable
architecture. Change the representation through a common cognitive interface.

## Hypothesis

Learned transition operators will transfer across fresh objects and compositions
and survive restarts. Complex channels may add predictive value, but must earn
their cost against strong classical alternatives.

## Unknown

Whether complex phases help; whether broader developmental competence emerges;
whether the earlier reported weight-edit results can be reproduced. No claim
that the v0.1 kernel autonomously invents meanings or general intelligence.
